## 🌀 Cyclone Fani (2019) — The Most Recent Major Cyclone to Hit Puri

### NDRF Teams Deployed (Post-Landfall)
**44 NDRF teams** were deployed across affected districts for Search & Rescue (SAR) and road clearance:
- **Puri: 14 teams** (highest allocation of any district)
- Bhubaneswar: 21, Jagatsinghpur: 2, Khordha: 2, Cuttack: 4, Balasore: 1

These teams collectively cleared **2,059 km of road**, removed **6,664 uprooted trees**, cleared **946 electric poles**, and provided **medical assistance to 1,448 persons**.
[Fani JRNA — Govt Annex 3: Deployment of Response Forces]

Additionally, **20 ODRAF units** and **245 Fire Teams** were deployed in support.

### Shelter Occupancy / Evacuees
**1,470,197 persons (1.47 million)** across 18 districts were evacuated and sheltered in **9,000 safe shelters** before landfall — described as *"the biggest preparedness mobilisation"* in Odisha's history.
- **24,889 tourists** were specifically moved to safety from Puri, Ganjam, Cuttack, and Balasore districts.
[UNICEF Fani SitRep #2, 2019]

> ⚠️ Note: District-level shelter occupancy figures specifically for Puri at "peak" are not broken out in the available documents — the 1.47 million figure is the **state-wide total**.
