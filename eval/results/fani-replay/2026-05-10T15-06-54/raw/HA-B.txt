## 🌀 SUFFICIENCY ASSESSMENT vs. CYCLONE PHAILIN (2013) — Last Major Cyclone on Record

Drawing from the **UNDP/DRM Survey Report on Cyclone Phailin (2013)**:

### Scale of the Event in Puri
- **~1,02,000 people evacuated** from Puri district alone during Phailin
- **1,81,915 people sheltered** in 489 safe shelters across the district
- Subsequent **post-cyclone flooding** in 3 districts required an additional **1,71,083 rescues**
- Evacuations had to begin **36 hours before landfall** — boats would have been needed in inundated areas immediately post-impact

### Were 27 Boats Sufficient? — Assessment

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| **Scale mismatch** | 27 boats for 1,00,000+ evacuees is a severe ratio — even running round-the-clock, each boat would need to ferry thousands of people |
| **Uneven distribution** | Brahmagiri (coastal, high-risk) had only 1 boat; NDRF/ODRAF boats had to supplement |
| **NDRF & ODRAF augmentation** | The DRM Survey confirms ODRAF (50 capacity, search/rescue trained) and NDRF (45 capacity) were deployed — civilian district boats alone were clearly inadequate |
| **15 additional NDRF teams** from other states were mobilised | Confirms state-level recognition that local resources were insufficient |
| **Helicopter requisition** | MI-17 helicopters from the Ministry of Defence were requested — indicating water/land rescue capacity was stretched |
| **Road vulnerability** | All relief lines to blocks noted risk of **tree falls during cyclones**, potentially cutting off road access and increasing dependency on boats for last-mile rescue |
| **Krushnaprasad VHF not installed** | ⚠️ Communication gap in one of the 3-boat blocks means boats may be deployed without reliable coordination |

### Key Gaps Identified
1. **27 boats are insufficient** for a district evacuating 1 lakh+ people — they function as a first-response layer, not the primary evacuation mechanism
2. **Brahmagiri's 1-boat allocation is a standing vulnerability** — reallocation from Puri Sadar or Kanas (4 each) should be pre-planned
3. **Satyabadi block has zero boats on record** — must be escalated for procurement or mutual aid agreement
4. **Satellite phones and NDRF/ODRAF pre-positioning** were critical force multipliers during Phailin and remain essential complements to district boats
5. The DDMP itself recommends *"escalation of mechanization of 100% DAT operated boats"* as a medium-term goal — acknowledging the current gap
