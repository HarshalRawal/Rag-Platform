## Storm Surge Heights — Severe Cyclones Near Puri/Odisha Coast

### 🌀 Cyclone Phailin (October 2013) — Most Relevant to Puri Region
According to the IMD post-cyclone survey report, the **maximum storm surge was 3.5 metres above astronomical tide** in the low-lying areas of **Ganjam district** of Odisha. The inland inundation of saline water extended up to approximately **1 kilometre from the coast**. Cyclone Phailin crossed the Odisha coast near **Gopalpur** (south of Puri) on **October 12, 2013** at around 2230 hrs IST, with sustained winds of **200–210 kmph gusting up to 220 kmph**. [Phailin_RDNA_2013]

### 🌀 Cyclone Dana (October 2024)
Cyclone Dana made landfall near **Dhamara** (north Odisha coast) in October 2024. The realised storm surge at landfall was **1–2 metres above astronomical tide**, inundating low-lying areas of **Kendrapara and Bhadrak districts**. Puri district was also listed among affected districts but the primary surge impact was to the north. [dana_bulletin_22, dana_bulletin_24]
