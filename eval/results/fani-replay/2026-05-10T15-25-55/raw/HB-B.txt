# 🎣 SDRF Compensation for a Fishing Household: Boat & Net Loss

## PART 1 — SDRF Norms Applicable to Fishermen

The SDRF norms (Odisha SDMP 2019, §9.2) under **Category 7: Fishery** specify the following assistance for repair/replacement of boats and nets:

| Item | Damage Type | SDRF Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Boat** (Dugout-Canoe / Catamaran) | Partially damaged | **Rs. 4,100** |
| **Boat** (Dugout-Canoe / Catamaran) | Fully damaged | **Rs. 9,600** |
| **Net** | Partially damaged | **Rs. 2,100** |
| **Net** | Fully damaged | **Rs. 2,600** |
| **Fish seed farm input subsidy** | Per hectare | **Rs. 8,200** |

> [Source: Odisha SDMP 2019, §9.2 — SDRF Fishery Norms, retrieved from Qdrant]

**⚠️ Important Conditions:**
- These amounts are **SDRF-funded** (state-level, automatic) and disbursed through the **respective District Collectors**.
- Assistance will **NOT** be provided if the beneficiary has **already availed of any subsidy or assistance** for the same calamity under any other Government scheme.
- Damage must be certified by a competent authority designated by the State Government.

### Additional Relief a Fishing Household May Claim (from SDRF Table 9.3):

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Clothing (if house washed away/fully damaged) | **Rs. 1,800** per family |
| Utensils/household goods (if house damaged) | **Rs. 2,000** per family |
| Livelihood assistance (not in relief camp) | **Rs. 60/adult/day + Rs. 45/child/day** (default 30 days) |

> [Table: `sdmp_table_9_3_sdrf_norms_of_assistance`, SDMP §9.3]

## PART 2 — Was This Relief Actually Disbursed to Fani-Affected Fishermen?

### 📋 What the Fani Relief Package Data Shows

The `relief_package` table (Fani-specific) **does not contain a dedicated fishermen boat/nets line item**. The relief disbursed to Fani-affected households was:

| Category | Districts | Rice | Cash |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extremely severely affected HH | Puri, parts of Khurdha | 50 kg | **Rs. 2,000** + polythene sheet |
| Severely affected HH | Khurdha | 1-month quota | **Rs. 1,000** + polythene sheet |
| Moderately affected HH | Cuttack, Kendrapada, Jagatsinghpur | 1-month quota | **Rs. 500** |

> [Table: `relief_package`, Fani Relief Package, page 5]

This means fishing households would have received the **general household cash + rice relief**, but the **boat/net-specific SDRF compensation** would have been handled separately via the Collectors under the SDMP §9.4 channel.

> [Source: Odisha SDMP 2019, §9.4; also Puri DDMP 2017-18]
