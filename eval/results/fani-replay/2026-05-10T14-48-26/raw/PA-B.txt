## 🏠 SDRF Norm: Fully Damaged Pucca House (Cyclone, Odisha)

| Location | SDRF Assistance |
|---|---|
| **Plain Areas** | **Rs. 95,100 per fully damaged/destroyed Pucca house** |
| Hilly Areas (incl. IAP districts) | Rs. 1,01,900 per fully damaged/destroyed Pucca house |

---

### Key Details

- **Fund Type:** SDRF (State Disaster Response Fund) — **state-funded**, with 75% Central share and 25% State share. This assistance is automatic and does not require central government (GOI) approval.
- **Applicable Disasters:** Cyclone is explicitly covered under SDRF-eligible calamities per MHA guidelines.
- **Eligibility:** The house must be **fully damaged or destroyed** (not partially or severely damaged, which have separate, lower norms).
- **Scale Ceiling:** Expenditure must remain within SDRF norms; any amount spent **above this ceiling** must be borne by the State Government from its own resources — it cannot be drawn from SDRF/NDRF.

---

### ⚠️ Important Distinctions (Do Not Confuse With)

| Item | Amount | It Is NOT House Damage |
|---|---|---|
| Clothing for families | Rs. 1,800 per family | Separate gratuitous relief item |
| Utensils/household goods | Rs. 2,000 per family | Separate gratuitous relief item |
| **Fully damaged Pucca house** | **Rs. 95,100** | ✅ This is the house damage norm |
