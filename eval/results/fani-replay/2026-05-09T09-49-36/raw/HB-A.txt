## 🎣 SDRF Compensation for a Fishing Household: Boats & Nets Lost

### PART 1 — SDRF Norms of Assistance (Fishery Sector)

The SDRF norms under **Section 9.2 of the Odisha SDMP 2019** (Item 7 — Fishery) prescribe the following for **repair/replacement of boats and nets damaged or lost** in a natural calamity:

| Asset | Damage Type | SDRF Assistance |
|---|---|---|
| **Boat** (Dugout-Canoe / Catamaran) | **Partially damaged** (repair) | **Rs. 4,100** per boat |
| **Boat** | **Fully damaged / lost** | **Rs. 9,600** per boat |
| **Net** | **Partially damaged** (repair) | **Rs. 2,100** per net |
| **Net** | **Fully damaged / lost** | **Rs. 2,600** per net |
| **Fish Seed Farm** | Input subsidy | **Rs. 8,200** per hectare |

📌 [Source: Odisha SDMP 2019, §9.2 — SDRF Items & Norms, Item 7: Fishery | **State Disaster Response Fund (SDRF)**]

> ⚠️ **Important conditions attached:**
> - This assistance is **NOT provided** if the fisherman has already availed of any subsidy or assistance for the same calamity under **any other Government scheme**.
> - Loss must be **certified by the competent authority** designated by the State Government.
> - SDRF assistance is **state-funded** and disbursed automatically without needing GoI (NDRF) approval.
