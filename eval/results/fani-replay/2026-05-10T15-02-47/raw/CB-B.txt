## 🌀 Cyclone Phailin (October 2013) — Figures for Puri & State

### NDRF Deployment
**28 NDRF teams** were pre-positioned across strategic locations statewide ahead of landfall. In addition, **15 extra NDRF teams were mobilised from other states** (beyond the 3rd Battalion based at Mundali), alongside **10 units of ODRAF**, plus Army, Navy, and Air Force teams on standby. [Phailin_UNDP_DRM_Survey_2013]

### People Evacuated / In Shelters
- **Puri district specifically: ~1,02,000 people** were evacuated due to Cyclone Phailin.
- **State total: ~9.84 lakh (983,642) people** were evacuated across 14 districts in the 36 hours preceding landfall — described as the **largest evacuation ever practised** at that time. [Phailin_UNDP_DRM_Survey_2013]
- In **Puri district**, **1,81,915 people were sheltered across 489 safe shelters**. [Phailin_UNDP_DRM_Survey_2013]
- All **160 MCS shelters in the affected area** were activated and stocked by 10 October 2013 with 2–3 days of essentials. [Phailin_UNDP_DRM_Survey_2013]
- Example at village level: **~1,000 people** took shelter in Arakhakuda MCS (Krushnaprasad Block, Puri), which has a capacity of 2,500. [Phailin_UNDP_DRM_Survey_2013]
