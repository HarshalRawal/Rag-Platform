## Evacuation Before Cyclone Fani Landfall

**Approximately 1.2 to 1.47 million people were evacuated** before Cyclone Fani made landfall on the Odisha coast near Puri at around 8:00–10:00 AM on **3 May 2019**. Different sources provide slightly varying figures:

| Source | Evacuees | Shelters Used |
|---|---|---|
| Fani Joint Rapid Needs Assessment (JRNA) | ~1.2 million | 4,000+ shelters, incl. 880 cyclone centres |
| UNICEF Fani Situation Report #2 | **1,47,0,197 persons (1.47 million)** | 9,000 safe shelters |
| European Commission ERCC Map (06 May 2019) | ~1.2 million | 4,000 cyclone shelters |

### Key Details of the Evacuation Operation:
- Evacuees were drawn from **vulnerable and low-lying areas across at least 11 coastal districts**.
- The operation involved **more than 45,000 volunteers, 2,000 emergency workers, and 1,00,000 officials**, alongside NDRF, ODRAF, Panchayati Raj Institutions, youth clubs, and civil society organisations. [UNICEF_Fani_SitRep_2_2019]
- **20 ODRAF teams and 525 fire teams** were deployed for the response. [UNICEF_Fani_SitRep_2_2019]
- **24,889 tourists** were also safely moved from Puri, Ganjam, Cuttack, and Balasore districts. [UNICEF_Fani_SitRep_2_2019]
- Cyclone warnings were disseminated via **Location-Based Alert System (LBAS) SMS** to 18 million subscribers and through **siren towers** in coastal areas. [UNICEF_Fani_SitRep_2_2019]

### Outcome:
This massive pre-landfall evacuation is widely credited with keeping casualties remarkably low — **64 deaths reported** (per the State Emergency Operation Centre, Bhubaneswar), despite Fani being a **Category 4 Very Severe Cyclonic Storm** with wind speeds of 175–185 km/h gusting to 210 km/h, affecting over **15 million people** including 4.8 million children. The **UN recognised this as a global good practice** for effective disaster preparedness and early warning. [UNICEF_Fani_SitRep_2_2019; Fani_JRNA]
